Neon has completed audits for SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2, SOC 3, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701, and adheres to GDPR and CCPA regulations. If your application handles protected health information, HIPAA compliance is available as a self-serve feature on the Scale plan. This page describes what each certification covers and how you can access compliance documentation.

## SOC 2

SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 audits are validated by independent auditors against the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) trust service criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

- **Type 1** confirms that controls are designed appropriately at a point in time.
- **Type 2** confirms that those controls operated effectively over an audit period.

## SOC 3

The SOC 3 report is a public-facing summary of the SOC 2 findings. It provides assurance to external parties without disclosing the detailed system description in a full SOC 2 report.

You can request the SOC 3 report through the [Neon Trust Center](https://trust.neon.com/) if it's available on your plan.

## ISO 27001

ISO 27001 is the internationally recognized standard for information security management systems (ISMS). Neon's certification demonstrates a systematic and risk-based approach to managing sensitive information.

## ISO 27701

ISO 27701 extends ISO 27001 to include data privacy requirements. It covers the establishment, implementation, and maintenance of a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) aligned with GDPR and other privacy regulations.

## GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) governs the collection, use, and storage of personal data for individuals in the European Union. Neon adheres to GDPR requirements, including support for data subject rights and cross-border data transfer mechanisms.

For privacy-related questions, contact [privacy@databricks.com](mailto:privacy@databricks.com).

## CCPA

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants California residents rights over their personal data. Neon complies with CCPA, including providing transparency about data collection and supporting user requests to access or delete personal information.

## HIPAA

HIPAA compliance is available as a self-serve feature for customers on the [Scale plan](/reference/plans). It enables applications handling Protected Health Information (PHI) to meet federal requirements.

<Note>
HIPAA support is currently available at no additional cost. When billing for HIPAA support begins, a 15% surcharge will be added to your monthly invoice. You'll be notified in advance before this change takes effect.
</Note>

Enabling HIPAA for your Neon organization and projects involves accepting a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), which defines Neon's responsibilities for protecting PHI. The BAA is available [here](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2025-08/baa-neon.pdf).

For full setup instructions, see [HIPAA compliance](/security/compliance).

### What HIPAA compliance includes

When HIPAA is enabled for a project, Neon activates:

- **Postgres audit logging** via the `pgaudit` extension, capturing all significant SQL activity including reads, writes, DDL changes, and role modifications.
- **Console and API audit logs** that record project management, branch operations, compute actions, and role changes.
- **Breach notification** within five business days of Neon becoming aware of a security incident.

### Non-HIPAA-compliant features

The following features must not be used in projects containing PHI:

- **Neon Auth** — Uses an authentication provider not covered under Neon's HIPAA compliance.
- **Data API** — Hosted outside Neon's HIPAA-compliant infrastructure.

## Data residency

When you create a Neon project, you choose a cloud provider (AWS or Azure) and a region. Your database data, including backups, remains in that region. Neon does not transfer data between regions unless you initiate it.

Available regions span the US, EU, and APAC. For the current list of supported regions, see the project creation page in the [Neon Console](https://console.neon.tech).

## Requesting compliance documents

Audit reports and other compliance documents can be requested through the [Neon Trust Center](https://trust.neon.com/).

</Tip>
