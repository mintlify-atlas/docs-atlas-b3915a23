## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a free account">
    Sign up at [console.neon.tech](https://console.neon.tech) — no credit card required. Your project is ready in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to your app">
    Copy your connection string from the Neon Console and plug it into your application. Neon is fully Postgres-compatible — no driver changes needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try branching">
    Create a database branch for your feature branch. Run schema migrations safely without touching production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale with confidence">
    Enable autoscaling and set a max compute size. Neon handles traffic spikes automatically so you never over-provision.
  </Step>
</Steps>
