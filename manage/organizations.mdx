In Neon, all projects live within organizations. When you sign up, Neon automatically creates a free organization for your first project. Organizations provide a central place to manage projects, collaborate with teammates, and handle billing. You can start inviting teammates as soon as your organization is created.

## User roles and permissions

Organizations have two member roles:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin">
    Admins have full control over the organization and all its projects. Admin capabilities include:

    - Create, update, and delete any project in the organization
    - Invite and remove members
    - Change member roles
    - Manage billing and plan settings
    - Transfer projects between organizations
    - Manage organization-level settings (IP Allow, protected branches, etc.)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Member">
    Members have access to all organization projects but cannot change org-level settings. Member capabilities include:

    - View and work with all projects in the organization
    - Create new projects (within plan limits)
    - Manage branches, databases, and roles within projects

    Members cannot delete projects or modify org settings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

For a complete breakdown of role permissions, see [User permissions](/manage/organizations).

## Set up an organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an organization">
    You can create additional organizations at any time. To create one, see [Create an organization](/manage/organizations).

    Your first organization is created automatically when you sign up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members">
    Go to the **People** page within your organization and click **Invite member**. Enter the email address of the person you want to add.

    For details, see [Invite members](/manage/organizations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles">
    When inviting a member, select whether they should be an **Admin** or **Member**. You can change roles at any time from the **People** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer projects (optional)">
    If you have existing personal projects, you can transfer them to your organization from the project's **Settings** > **Transfer** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto-provision members by email domain

You can configure your organization to automatically add new users whose email matches a verified domain. When a user with a matching email signs up or logs in, they are added to the organization automatically.

For setup instructions, see [Add members by domain](/manage/organizations).

## Organization billing

Paid plan billing is managed at the organization level. All charges for projects within an organization appear on a single invoice for that organization.

To access billing for your organization:

1. Navigate to the Neon Console.
2. Select your organization from the breadcrumb menu at the top-left.
3. Select **Billing** from the menu.

For details on plans and costs, see [Neon plans, pricing, and billing](/manage/billing).

## Current limitations

- **Branch management**: All users can currently manage [protected branches](/manage/branches) regardless of their role. Granular permissions for this feature are not yet implemented.
- **Permissions granularity**: The current role system may not meet all needs for fine-grained access control.
