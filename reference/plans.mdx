Neon offers three main plans to match different stages of development and production needs. You start for free and pay only for what you use as your workload grows. This page describes each plan, the resources included, and how billing works for each usage dimension.

<Note>
Invoices under $0.50 are not collected. On the Free plan, compute suspends when plan limits are reached. Upgrade to a paid plan to continue.
</Note>

## Free plan

The Free plan is designed for prototypes, side projects, and small teams. It includes everything you need to build and test an application at no cost.

<Note>
The Free plan always suspends inactive computes after 5 minutes. You cannot disable Scale to Zero on the Free plan.
</Note>

**What's included:**

- 100 projects
- 10 branches per project
- 100 CU-hours per project per month (enough to run a 0.25 CU compute for ~400 hours/month)
- 0.5 GB storage per project
- 5 GB public network transfer per month
- 6-hour restore window (up to 1 GB-month of change history)
- 1 manual snapshot
- Autoscaling up to 2 CU (8 GB RAM)
- Up to 60,000 monthly active users for Neon Auth
- 1 day of monitoring metrics history

When compute or transfer limits are reached, your compute suspends until the next billing period or until you upgrade.

## Launch plan

The Launch plan is for startups and growing teams that need more resources, production-ready features, and flexibility. You pay only for what you use.

**Pricing:**

- Compute: **$0.106/CU-hour**
- Storage: **$0.35/GB-month**
- Instant restore storage: **$0.20/GB-month**
- Extra branches: **$1.50/branch-month** (prorated hourly)
- Public network transfer: 100 GB included, then **$0.10/GB**

**What's included:**

- 100 projects
- 10 branches per project (up to 5,000 with extras)
- Autoscaling up to 16 CU (64 GB RAM)
- Scale to Zero (can be disabled for always-on computes)
- Up to 7-day restore window
- 10 manual snapshots
- Protected branches
- Up to 1M monthly active users for Neon Auth
- 3 days of monitoring metrics history
- Billing support

## Scale plan

The Scale plan is for production-grade workloads requiring higher limits, advanced security, compliance certifications, and SLAs. You pay only for what you use.

<Note>
The higher per-CU-hour rate on Scale reflects included compliance certifications, security features, and uptime SLAs that are not available on Launch.
</Note>

**Pricing:**

- Compute: **$0.222/CU-hour**
- Storage: **$0.35/GB-month**
- Instant restore storage: **$0.20/GB-month**
- Extra branches: **$1.50/branch-month** (prorated hourly)
- Public network transfer: 100 GB included, then **$0.10/GB**
- Private network transfer: **$0.01/GB**

**What's included:**

- 1,000 projects (soft limit; request more via support)
- 25 branches per project (up to 5,000 with extras)
- Autoscaling up to 16 CU; fixed computes up to 56 CU (224 GB RAM)
- Scale to Zero fully configurable (1 minute to always on)
- Up to 30-day restore window
- 10 manual snapshots
- Metrics and Postgres log export (Datadog, OpenTelemetry)
- Protected branches, IP Allow, Private Networking (AWS PrivateLink)
- SOC 2, SOC 3, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, GDPR, CCPA compliance
- HIPAA compliance (available as an add-on)
- Uptime SLA
- Up to 1M monthly active users for Neon Auth
- 14 days of monitoring metrics history
- Standard support (Business and Production support available for additional fee)

## Key billing concepts

### Compute hours

Compute usage is measured in CU-hours: `compute size (CU) × hours actively running`. Scale to Zero suspends idle computes, so you only pay for active time.

### Storage

- **Root branches:** billed on actual logical data size.
- **Child branches:** billed on the minimum of accumulated changes since creation or the logical data size. You never pay more than your actual data size.
- Storage is metered hourly and billed per GB-month.

### Instant restore storage

Neon retains WAL history to support point-in-time restore. This history is stored only for root branches. You are billed based on how much change history you retain, not the number of restores you perform. Shorten your restore window to reduce this cost.

### Snapshots

Snapshots capture a complete point-in-time state of a root branch. Snapshots are free during the Beta period. Starting May 1, 2026, snapshot storage is billed at **$0.09/GB-month**.

### Extra branches

Branches beyond your plan allowance are billed at $1.50/branch-month, prorated hourly (~$0.002/hour).

## Upgrading or downgrading

To change your plan:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to billing settings">
    In the Neon Console, open your organization settings and select **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Choose the plan you want to move to. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Review the pricing and confirm. You will see a prorated charge or credit on your next invoice for plan changes mid-month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
When downgrading to a lower plan, ensure your usage falls within the limits of that plan before the change takes effect. For example, if you have more than 10 branches per project on Launch and try to move to the Free plan, excess branches will need to be deleted.
</Warning>

## Cost optimization

A few practices that reduce your Neon costs:

- Enable Scale to Zero so idle computes don't accumulate CU-hours.
- Set a maximum autoscaling size to cap peak compute usage.
- Delete unused branches to reduce storage costs.
- Shorten your restore window if you don't need the full history depth.
- Set time-to-live policies on development and preview branches.

See the [cost optimization guide](/manage/billing) for detailed strategies.
